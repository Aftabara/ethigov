Why Ethigov

Researchers work in separate networks

Early-career scholars struggle to find mentors. Projects that need law, policy, computing and education expertise find it hard to assemble the right team. Ethigov gives these people one place to find each other and work together.

  • Co-author research papers
  • Build international research teams
  • Find and apply for grants
  • Run surveys
  • Publish white papers
  • Develop policy recommendations
  • Mentor future scholars
  • Contribute to responsible AI governance

About

Research that reaches policy

Ethigov is a not-for-profit initiative that brings scholars, policy experts, institutions and funders together around responsible AI governance.

Vision and mission

What we are working toward

Vision. To be a trusted collaborative platform for AI governance research and policy innovation.

Mission. To enable collaboration between established scholars, policy experts, doctoral researchers, early-career academics and institutions, so that rigorous research can inform responsible AI governance around the world.

Values

Principles we work by

Academic excellence

We keep to rigorous scholarly standards.

Transparency

Collaboration is open and accountable.

Inclusion

Researchers from every region take part on equal terms.

Public good

Research is designed to benefit society.

Innovation

We look for new, practical ways to govern AI responsibly.
